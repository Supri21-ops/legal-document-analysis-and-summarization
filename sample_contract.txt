MASTER SOFTWARE SERVICES AND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

This Master Software Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into and made effective as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between:

Client: Nexus Global Enterprises Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 500 Technology Parkway, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Client"), and

Vendor: Apex Cloud Solutions LLC, a California limited liability company with its principal place of business at 1200 Innovation Way, Austin, TX 78701 ("Vendor" or "Contractor").

Client and Vendor may collectively be referred to as the "Parties" or individually as a "Party."

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Vendor shall provide custom cloud architecture design, enterprise software development, and AI pipeline integration services as detailed in Statement of Work #1 ("SOW"). Vendor agrees to perform all work in accordance with industry best practices and within the project milestones specified.

2. TERM AND DURATION
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date (January 15, 2026) and shall continue in full force and effect for an initial term of twelve (12) months, expiring on January 14, 2027, unless terminated earlier in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
Client agrees to pay Vendor a total contract sum of $150,000 USD for the deliverables. Payments shall be made in monthly installments of $12,500 USD within thirty (30) days of receipt of an undisputed invoice. Any late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE
Each Party agrees that all confidential information, including proprietary trade secrets, business strategies, customer lists, and software source code disclosed during the term of this Agreement shall remain strictly confidential. The receiving Party shall not disclose such information to any third party without prior written consent, and such confidentiality obligation shall survive for five (5) years following termination.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
Client shall own all rights, title, and interest, including all patent, copyright, and trademark rights, in and to all deliverables, custom software, and documentation created by Vendor specifically under this Agreement as "work-made-for-hire." Vendor retains ownership of its pre-existing background intellectual property, tools, and proprietary frameworks.

6. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PARTIES
Vendor shall dedicate qualified engineering personnel and maintain weekly progress reporting. Client agrees to provide timely feedback within five (5) business days of milestone submissions and grant appropriate system access for testing and deployment.

7. INDEMNIFICATION AND UNLIMITED LIABILITY (HIGH RISK)
Vendor agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client, its affiliates, directors, and employees against any and all third-party claims, liabilities, damages, and legal costs. Vendor accepts unlimited liability and shall remain personally liable for any material breach, data loss, security compromise, or infringement claim without limitation of liability or financial cap whatsoever.

8. TERMINATION AND CANCELLATION (HIGH RISK)
Client may terminate this Agreement immediately without notice at its sole discretion for convenience at any time. Upon such immediate termination, Vendor shall forfeit all rights to upcoming milestone payments, and Client shall owe no further compensation or cancellation penalty. Either Party may terminate upon material breach if uncured after seven (7) days written notice.

9. NON-COMPETE RESTRICTION (HIGH RISK)
Vendor and its key personnel agree that during the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination, Vendor shall not engage in, consult for, or provide similar enterprise software development services to any direct competitor of Client in North America or Europe.

10. WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMER
Vendor warrants that all software delivered shall perform substantially in conformance with specifications for a period of ninety (90) days following acceptance. Except as expressly stated herein, all deliverables are provided as-is, and Vendor disclaims all other implied warranties of merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

11. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither Party shall be held liable for failure or delay in performing obligations due to causes beyond its reasonable control, including acts of God, flood, earthquake, war, government restrictions, or nationwide power grid outages.

12. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of law principles. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in New York, NY, and both Parties explicitly waive all rights to a trial by jury.
